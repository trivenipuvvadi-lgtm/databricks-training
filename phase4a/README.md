
# Phase 4A – Customer Segmentation

## Overview

This phase focuses on customer segmentation using **SQL** and **PySpark** in **Databricks Community Edition**.

The project analyzes customer spending and classifies customers into different segments based on their total purchase amount.

---

## Platform

- Databricks Community Edition

---

## Dataset

- Sales Dataset

---

## Business Tasks

### Task 1 – Customer Segmentation

Calculate total spending for each customer and classify them into:

- Gold
- Silver
- Bronze

using conditional logic.

### Task 2 – Segment Distribution

Count the number of customers in each segment.

### Task 3 – Quantile-Based Segmentation

Calculate spending quantiles using:

- approxQuantile()

Analyze customer spending distribution.
